# Why Your August Payouts Didn't Match Your Sales

Your Shopify store generated **EUR 2,819.82** in card sales in August, but your bank account received only **EUR 2,448.74**. Here's where the missing **EUR 371.08** went:

## The Complete Breakdown

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Card orders total | EUR 2,819.82 |
| Less: Refunds | EUR 70.50 |
| Less: Card processing fees | EUR 56.59 |
| Less: Dispute amounts | EUR 19.30 |
| Less: Dispute fees | EUR 15.00 |
| Less: Payments still in transit | EUR 209.69 |
| **Paid out in August** | **EUR 2,448.74** |

## What Happened to Each Missing EUR

**EUR 70.50 - Refunds**  
Customers returned this amount in merchandise refunds during August. This reduces your payout directly.

**EUR 56.59 - Card Processing Fees**  
Shopify Payments charged this to process card transactions. This is your cost of accepting cards.

**EUR 34.30 - Chargeback Dispute**  
One customer (Order #1060) disputed a EUR 19.30 charge, and Shopify applied a EUR 15.00 dispute fee on top. The entire EUR 34.30 came out of your payout.

**EUR 209.69 - Payments Still in Transit**  
Four orders weren't paid out until early September (after month end):
- Order #1050: EUR 43.19 (paid Sept 1)
- Order #1059: EUR 90.37 (paid Sept 1)  
- Order #1009: EUR 42.50 (paid Sept 2)
- Order #1058: EUR 33.63 (paid Sept 2)

These will show up in your September bank statement.

## One More Thing: Non-Shopify Payment

Order #1055 for EUR 99.00 was paid via PayPal Express Checkout, not Shopify Payments. This amount doesn't appear in your Shopify Payments payout report at all—it goes directly to your PayPal account instead.

---

**Bottom line:** The EUR 371.08 difference is fully explained by refunds, fees, disputes, and timing. Everything adds up perfectly to EUR 2,448.74.
