Every cent of it is accounted for below. About two thirds of it is not lost: some is still on its way, some went to PayPal, and some was never real money.

## What each item means

**1. Not lost, only arriving later: €209.69.** Four orders from Aug 30–31 (#1050, #1059, #1009, #1058, €213.90 gross, €4.21 fees) were still "in transit" when the export was made. They pay out on September 1 and 2. They should appear in your bank in early September.

**2. Not lost, only in a different place: €99.00.** Order #1055 was paid with PayPal. That money is in your PayPal account, not in your Shopify payouts. Check your PayPal balance. PayPal takes its own fee, so the amount there will be a little less than €99.00.

**3. Never real money: €103.80.**
- **#1013 (€80.90)** is tagged "test" and has no card charge. It makes your sales report look higher than it is. **Please check one thing:** the order is marked *fulfilled*. If a real parcel was shipped for it, you gave away €80.90 of goods without being paid. If it was only a test, cancel or archive it so it stops counting in your reports.
- **#1021 (€22.90)** was cancelled on Aug 16. The card authorization was voided, so no money moved.

**4. Actually gone: €161.39.**
- **Processing fees: €56.59.** Every charge cost exactly 1.5% + €0.25, so the rate was applied consistently. This total includes the fees on the refunded and disputed orders (€1.16 on #1042 and €0.54 on #1060), because Shopify keeps the fee when you refund.
- **Refunds: €70.50.** #1042 was refunded in full (€60.50) and #1030 in part (€10.00).
- **Chargeback on #1060: €34.30.** The customer disputed the €19.30 charge on Aug 18. Shopify took the €19.30 back and charged a €15.00 dispute fee. The dispute cost almost twice the value of the order. In your orders export this order still shows as "paid", so it is easy to miss. Open the order in Shopify admin, or find the Disputes list under your Shopify Payments settings, to see its status. If the dispute is still open, you can submit evidence (for example tracking and proof of delivery). If you win, the €19.30 comes back.

## One thing that may look odd on your bank statement

The payout on **Aug 26 (PO-20260826) is negative: −€60.50.** That is the #1042 refund. There were no sales that day to cover it, so Shopify took the money from your bank account instead of sending money in. That is normal, but it shows up as a debit.

## Worth knowing (not part of the gap)

Your prices include VAT. The order totals contain **€482.58 of tax**, which is part of the money you receive but not money you keep. So even after the payouts, the €3,022.62 figure is well above your real revenue. Some of that tax is on the test, voided and refunded orders, so your accountant should work from the actual paid amounts.
