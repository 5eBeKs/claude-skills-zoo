### What the owner can see

A right answer can rest on a basis the owner never chose: a payout gap measured from every order in the export, test and cancelled ones included; a margin given per unit this month and for the month the next; pre-orders counted as sales when the owner counts a sale once it ships. Each is correct arithmetic, and next month's answer to the same question may use another. A different basis is not a wrong answer: on store C, Opus without the plugin spread discounts and refunds over the products and said so. The trouble is that it is chosen afresh each time; for the same margins question it was per unit on store A, for the month on store B, and after discounts on store C. Three readings of the kept answers, no model involved:

- **on the skill's basis**: the answer prints the figure the store's definitions and the skill's stated basis give;
- **everything named**: every order or product the results list for the owner (left out and why, disputes, not in payouts, in transit, below cost, no cost) is named in the answer, checked by the same coverage check the skills run, with the scripts and checks of the commit the answer was made with (a later version lists more, and an answer cannot name what its version did not list);
- **by hand**: the answer says its figures were added up by hand, or that it could not run code, so the owner should check them before use.
