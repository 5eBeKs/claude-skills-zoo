**How Shopify counts these:**
- **Dating:** sales are dated by when the order was placed (Amsterdam time). Refunds and cancellations are dated by when they happened. So some July and August orders refunded in September reduce September, and September orders refunded in October don't.
- **Prices include VAT:** Shopify takes the VAT out and shows it on its own line.
- **Included:**
  - All sales channels: web shop, the Utrecht showroom (124 orders), B2B draft orders (30 orders, €21.2k), and an unnamed app channel with ID 3890849 (332 orders, €29.8k).
  - Unpaid orders: 46 September orders still owe €13.2k (bank transfer, Klarna and Net 30 invoices).
  - Orders paid with gift cards.
- **Cancellations:** cancelled orders count as a sale on the order date and are reversed on the cancellation date. For example, 11 August orders cancelled in September reduce September by €706.
- **Excluded:**
  - Gift card sales: €8,100 (these count once a card is spent).
  - Tips: €26.50.
  - Chargebacks and the €5,000 Shopify Payments reserve.
  - Payment fees.
- **Payouts won't match sales:** payouts only cover card and iDEAL payments, not PayPal, Klarna or bank transfer, and they arrive later.

**One gap:** only card and iDEAL refunds have dates in the exports. PayPal, Klarna and bank-transfer refunds (€22.5k across all months) have no dates. Based on how quickly the dated refunds happen, about **€9.4k more** probably belongs in September. That would bring net sales to about **€591.8k** and total sales to about **€753.4k**. The exact figure is in Shopify under Analytics → Reports → "Sales over time" for September. That report is the one to give the bookkeeper if the numbers need to match exactly.
