**€351,295.07 reached the bank from Shopify Payments in August.** That's the 18 payouts marked `paid` and dated 3–27 August. Your August orders came to **€690,240.21**, which leaves a gap of €338,945.

Nearly three quarters of that gap isn't lost money. Those sales were paid through channels whose money never comes through Shopify payouts. Most of the rest is timing, plus a failed payout and a reserve that are still worth following up.

### Where the rest went

1. **Other payment channels: about €246k.** This is the biggest piece. PayPal, Klarna, bank transfers, B2B invoices and cash settle separately (PayPal balance, Klarna settlements, direct bank transfers). Look for them under those names in the bank statement, not under Shopify. The figures include €3,117 of voided orders and €8,464 of refunded orders, so the true amount received through these channels is somewhat lower.
2. **Timing: net about €38k.** About €79k of late-August sales were paid out in September. That's partly offset by €41k of late-July sales that were paid out in August.
3. **Failed payout: €14,902.03.** The 28 August payout has status `failed`, so it never arrived. Shopify added it to the 2 September payout (€76,846.71, which shows it as the retried amount). A failed payout usually means the bank rejected it. It's worth confirming the bank details are right and that the 2 September payout really arrived.
4. **Reserve: €7,500.** Shopify withheld this from the 10 August payout, which is why that payout was only €5,741.66. Only **€5,000 was released, on 21 September**. That leaves **€2,500 still held by Shopify**. You can see this in the reserve status in Shopify Payments.
5. **Normal deductions: about €29k.** This covers €20.0k of refunds, €8.4k of fees and €0.7k of chargebacks. Most refunds paid out in August were for July orders.

A few things to keep in mind:
- **What I counted as "sales":** I used order totals, including VAT and shipping, for orders created in August (Amsterdam time). Shopify's own sales report will differ by the refunds and voided orders.
- **Payout date vs. bank date:** I counted payouts by their payout date. Bank arrival can lag a day or two, so the 31 July payout (€15,827.23) may show on your August statement. If you match to the bank statement, move it or the 27 August payout between months as needed.
- **A small discrepancy:** Three orders (#63757, #63758 and #63765, €239.55 in total) are marked paid through Shopify Payments but have no charge in the transactions export. That's small, but worth a look.
