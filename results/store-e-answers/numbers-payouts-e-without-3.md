**Where the rest went:**

1. **Other payment methods, €248k: most of the gap and expected.** PayPal (€116.5k), Klarna (€82.8k), bank transfer (€23.0k), manual/invoice orders (€15.2k), cash (€2.3k) and gift cards (about €7.7k) never pass through Shopify Payments. PayPal and Klarna pay out on their own, so check those deposits separately. This figure also includes a small amount of voided or still-unpaid orders, such as about €1.1k of outstanding balances.
2. **Timing, net −€38k.** Late-August sales landed in September, partly offset by late-July sales landing in August.
3. **The 28 August payout of €14,902.03 failed.** It was retried and paid on 2 September, inside the €76,846.71 payout. It arrived, just in September. Ask your bank or Shopify why it bounced (a change of bank details is a common cause), because it could happen again.
4. **Shopify's reserve: €7,500 held on 10 August.** €5,000 was released on 21 September, so **€2,500 is still held**. Check Shopify Payments → Payouts for the reason and release date.
5. **Refunds, fees and chargebacks, about €29k:** ordinary running costs. Fee rates were steady at about 2.15% for cards and Apple Pay and about 0.3% for iDEAL. Payments in GBP, USD or CHF cost about 4.6% because of the currency conversion fee, which is worth knowing but hasn't changed.
