**Things I had to work around:**
- **Renamed codes:** 312 July order lines for the Oat and Sage napkins use their old product codes. The Handthrown Bowl has no product codes. I matched both by product name.
- **VAT:** Swiss and US orders are priced without VAT, while EU and UK prices include it. I handled each case accordingly.
- **Costs are today's:** the costs come from today's products export. If a cost changed since July, that product's figure will be off.
- **Missing fees:** card fees cover Shopify Payments only. PayPal and Klarna fees aren't in the files, so orders paid that way look slightly better than they were.
- **Refunds:** the export doesn't say which item in an order was refunded, so I split each refund across the order's items. Whether returned goods went back into stock or not moves profits by only a few percent and doesn't change any verdict.
