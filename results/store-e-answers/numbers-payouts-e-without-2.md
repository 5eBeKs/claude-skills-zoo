### What to act on
1. **Other payment channels (about €246k).** This is the biggest part of the gap, but the money isn't lost. It should be in your PayPal and Klarna payouts, direct bank transfers and cash, so check those separately. The B2B invoices (€15.2k) are worth checking too, because one of them (€1,078.46) is still marked unpaid.
2. **Failed payout of €14,902.03 on 28 Aug.** The bank rejected it. Shopify added it to the 2 September payout (€76,846.71), so it did arrive. But a payout usually fails because of a problem with the bank account details, so check whether that's fixed.
3. **Reserve.** On 10 August Shopify held back €7,500. It released €5,000 on 21 September, so **€2,500 is still being held** by Shopify.
4. **Timing (−€72.9k + €37.5k).** Shopify pays out a few days after each sale, so sales from the end of one month arrive in the next. This evens out over time.
5. **Fees, refunds and chargebacks (about €32k)** are normal costs.

The payout date in the export is the day Shopify sends the money, so the bank may credit it a day or two later. If you're matching against a bank statement, the payout from 27 August might show up there in early September.
