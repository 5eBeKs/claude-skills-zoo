**Customers:** 1,548 were paying at Sep 30, up from 1,403 at Aug 31. We lost 58.

**Billings** were $341,477.31. That includes the $52,260 enterprise invoice that is still open, and 15 negative invoices from downgrades. It leaves out $2,473.35 of invoices that were finalized in September and have since been voided.

**Cash collected** was $330,573.95. Of that, $65,480.04 came from two enterprise bank transfers and the rest from card charges. I left out two test-mode payments ($1,008) and all failed attempts.

**Refunds** were $338.88, from 10 refunds. No refund failed in September; the one failed refund was in August.

**Stripe fees** were $12,802.04:
- $10,126.15 on charges.
- $60 on disputes.
- $2,600.89 in Billing and Tax usage fees.
- $15 net of dispute-countered fees ($45 charged, $30 given back).

**Paid out to the bank** was $277,424.98, from the payouts arriving Sep 3, 10, 17 and 24. The $72,505.74 payout created Sep 30 arrives Oct 1, so it belongs to October. Stripe's September balance summary shows $349,930.72 of payouts because it includes that one.

**Revenue recognised** was $228,760.10: $230,970.98 from invoice lines less $2,210.88 of credit notes. Deferred revenue at Sep 30 was $593,812.15, mostly the annual plans and enterprise contracts.

## Things to know about these numbers
- **Subscription changes with no date:** 41 subscriptions had seat or plan changes after their last invoice, and those changes hadn't been invoiced by Oct 3, so the export doesn't show when they happened. For 39 of them, a September date is more likely, so I counted their Oct 3 settings at Sep 30. If you count the old settings for all of them instead, Sep 30 MRR would be $220,480.75, which is $493.94 higher.
- **Retention coupon dates:** for 9 subscriptions the export doesn't show exactly when the 25%-off retention coupon (rT5wYp0Q) started or ended. I applied each one to whichever side of the month-end was more likely. Each case is under $40 of MRR.
- **Subscriptions renewing on Oct 1 UTC:** Oct 1 00:00 UTC is 8 pm on Sep 30 in New York. Those renewals count at Sep 30, and subscriptions set to cancel at that moment count as churned in September.
- **Paused after trial:** 12 subscriptions were paused when their trial ended and resumed later. They count zero while paused.
- **Credit notes near month-end:** the Sep 30 9:50 pm credit note ($31.68) falls in September. The Oct 2 one falls in October.
