**Judgement calls that affect the numbers**
- **Rebuilding past months:** the subscription export only shows October 3. I rebuilt each subscription's price and quantity at each month end from its invoice lines, then checked the result against the October 3 export. The only differences were changes made after the last invoice.
- **Coupon timing:** I assumed a limited-time coupon starts running when the subscription is created, even during a trial. That is how Stripe does it. This moves 5 customers' July MRR by $335 in total. The biggest is a $176 partner "3 months free" plan: I count its free period as ended by July 31, so it isn't new MRR in August.
- **Migrated customers:** 5 customers moved over from the old billing system had trials in Stripe until their old renewal dates. They count zero while in trial, so their first MRR in August shows as **new**, even though they were already long-standing customers.
- **Subscription with no recent invoices:** one active EUR subscription has no invoice since July 8, which looks like paused collection. I counted it at €326 a month from its last invoice.
- **Credit note timing:** the exports don't give a service period for credit notes. I spread each one over the period of the invoice it credits.
