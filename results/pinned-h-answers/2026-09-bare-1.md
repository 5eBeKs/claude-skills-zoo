How I counted:
- **Enterprise contracts are included.** Seven customers are billed by manual PO invoices outside Stripe subscriptions, and together they are about $22.5k of MRR. Sevhux ($48k a year less an agreed 10% discount, so $3,600/month) started Sep 1 and counts as new. Silvelwim's £20,000 contract starts Oct 1, so it is not in September's MRR. Leaving these contracts out would understate MRR by roughly 10%.
- **What's excluded or reduced:**
  - Trials and paused subscriptions count as zero.
  - Discounts that are currently running reduce MRR. That includes 100% partner and friends coupons, and annual plans that were billed at 50% off under the launch offer.
  - One-time coupons and one-off fees are not MRR.
  - Annual plans are divided by 12.
  - Sales tax is excluded.
- **Currency:** EUR and GBP are converted at Sep 30 rates (1.1719 and 1.3587) at both dates, so the movement doesn't include exchange-rate swings.
- **Past-due customers are included.** 26 customers ($3.6k MRR) had an unpaid renewal in dunning on Sep 30.
