**Judgement calls that change the numbers:**
1. **Changes visible only in the October 3 export.** 36 subscriptions show a different seat count, and three show a switch from Business plus seats to Team, with no invoice yet. Their credit or charge will only appear on the next renewal invoice, so the exact change date is unknown. For all but three of them, most of the possible window falls in September, so I counted all 39 as done by September 30. That lowers September MRR by $484.94; if you'd rather count only what's been invoiced, MRR is $220,480.74 and net new is +$22,724.66.
2. **Migrated annual customers are counted as trials.** About 30 subscriptions tagged `migrated_from: legacy_billing` sit in Stripe as "trialing" until their old renewal date, even though they probably prepaid in the legacy system. Your rule counts trials as zero, so they're left out: $2,787.15 at September 30 and $3,296.29 at August 31. As each one reaches its renewal it will show up as new MRR.
3. **16 subscriptions were in Stripe's "unpaid" status on August 31.** Their payment retries had failed months earlier, later invoices were never charged, and they were all cancelled together on September 30. I counted them as zero on August 31, so they aren't in September's churn.
4. **Retention-coupon start dates are estimated.** The export doesn't record when the 25%-off retention coupon was applied, only that it started between two invoices. Where that window crosses a month end (four subscriptions), I assumed the more likely side.
