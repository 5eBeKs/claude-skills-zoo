**Readings I took** (they appear as open questions in every answer until you confirm them):
- **Orders:** each Amazon order id placed in the month (Pacific time) counts once. An order is left out only if every line of it was cancelled.
- **One report per figure:** sales, refunds, fees, advertising and reimbursements come from the monthly transaction report. What is owed and the reserve come from the settlement files, deposits from your bank's list, and orders from the orders report.
- **Carried balances:** a failed deposit or a negative settlement that Amazon carries into a later settlement is matched to it by amount and counted once.
- **Deposit dates:** a deposit dated the last day of the month counts in that month, because the bank list has dates but no times.
