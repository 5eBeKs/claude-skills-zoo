**How I counted:**
- **July:** orders placed July 1–31, store time: 7,882 orders. I left out 145 that were cancelled, voided or test orders.
- **VAT:** UK and EU prices include VAT, so I took it out before counting sales.
- **Refunds:**
  - **Which item:** the export only says which item was refunded on about 1% of refunds. I matched refund amounts to specific items, which worked for about 90% of refunded orders. The rest are shipping refunds or small goodwill credits, and I spread those across the order.
  - **Returned stock:** I assumed refunded items weren't put back into stock, because Shopify doesn't mark them as restocked. If they were resold, each product's profit rises by a few hundred dollars.
- **Fees:** Shopify Payments fees are the actual amounts from the transactions file. That file has no PayPal fees, so for PayPal orders (about 6%) I used PayPal's standard rate of 3.49% + $0.49.
- **Old SKUs:** Classic Crew Tee sales under the old `CT-` SKUs are counted with the tee.
- **Costs:** these are the product export's current "Cost per item" figures. If your costs were different in July, the margins would change a little.
