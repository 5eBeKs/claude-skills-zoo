## In short
- **About $84k** was never meant to come through Shopify Payments. PayPal, wholesale invoices, cash and gift cards need checking against their own accounts. The $776 of test orders shouldn't count as sales at all.
- **About $110k** was real money that stayed with Shopify: $83k in refunds, $26k in fees and $1.5k in chargebacks.
- **About $12k** was net timing. Roughly $117k of late-June money landed on July 7, including the failed payout. This was mostly offset by $105k of May sales that landed in early June.
- **$8,227 is unexplained.** In 192 card orders, the amount charged is lower than the order total, often by exactly one item's price. For example, order #55847 totals $130 but was charged $102. Nothing in these exports accounts for it. It's worth checking the payment history of a few of these orders in Shopify admin.
- **Separate from June:** the fix for the failed June 30 payout should be checked with the bank. Also, Shopify moved $25,000 into a reserve (held back) on August 21 and returned only $10,000 on September 4.

I counted "sales" as order totals by order date in Eastern time, which is the export's timezone. Shopify's own sales report subtracts refunds and taxes differently, so its figure won't match $788k exactly.
