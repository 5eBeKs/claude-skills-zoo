**Where your definitions left something open, this is what I decided** (each appears in every monthly answer until you confirm or change it):
- **Unpaid vs past_due:** a subscription counts as past_due, and in MRR, while Stripe is still retrying a failed invoice. It becomes unpaid (zero) after the last retry (on this account, the first try plus four retries) and stays unpaid until its next successful payment. 16 subscriptions were unpaid at July 31.
- **Customers moved over from your old billing system:** when their migration trial ended in July, I counted them as new, because their MRR history only exists in Stripe. There are 8 of them, $316.75 of the new MRR, shown on its own line.
- **Payout arrival in New York time:** a payout arriving at midnight UTC on August 1 arrived on the evening of July 31 in New York, so it counts in July. This matches Stripe's own July balance summary.
- **Stripe's monthly Billing and Tax usage fees:** they count in the month Stripe charged them. So July's fees include June's usage fee.
- **Credit notes:** they do not reduce billings, which stay at invoice totals. Any money they sent back counts in refunds, and they reduce revenue over the service periods they credit. This matters in September, when a $5,226 credit note was issued against an enterprise invoice.
- **Coupons:** one used at signup runs from when the subscription was created. A coupon added later (the retention offer) runs from the first invoice that shows it.
