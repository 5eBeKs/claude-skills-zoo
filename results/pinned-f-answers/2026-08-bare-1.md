# Stripe month-end for August 2026

All amounts are in USD, taken from the balance transactions export. Stripe had already converted the EUR, GBP and CAD charges to dollars. I split the month using the account's own timezone, which the export shows as UTC−5, so August means Aug 1 00:00 to Aug 31 23:59 local time. Test-mode payments and failed payments never reached the balance, so they aren't counted.

## What we sold
- **4,266 charges came to $139,509.28.** This includes $8,402.50 of sales tax, VAT and GST.
- **104 refunds took back $3,204.12.**
- **10 disputes withdrew $289.21.** 2 disputes from July were won, which returned $53.45.
- **Net volume was $136,069.40.** This is before Stripe's fees.

## What Stripe kept: $7,302.22
- Card processing fees: $6,408.26
- Dispute fees: $150.00 charged, less $30.00 returned on the won disputes, so $120.00
- Stripe Billing usage fees: $420.20
- Stripe Tax usage fees: $353.76

**Rolling reserve:** starting Aug 24, Stripe withheld 5% of each day's volume. By month end it held $1,671.14. The first release is due around Sept 23, 30 days after the first withholding. This money is still ours but can't be paid out yet, so the bookkeeper should record it as a receivable from Stripe, not as a fee.

## What reached the bank
- **$119,805.62 arrived in the bank during August, in 18 payouts.** This includes the July 31 payout, which landed on Aug 3.
- **$10,085.69 was in transit at month end.** This payout was created Aug 31 and arrived Sept 1.
- **The Aug 13 payout of $3,688.05 failed** because the bank account number was invalid. Stripe put it back in the balance on Aug 14, and it went out again as part of the Aug 18 payout of $31,351.45. It should not be counted twice.

The bank side also ties out: $3,552.26 in transit at Jul 31, plus $126,339.05 of payouts, less $10,085.69 in transit at Aug 31, equals the $119,805.62 that arrived.

## Things for the bookkeeper to note
1. **A Stripe Billing fee was charged twice on Aug 10.** The extra $11.51 is included in August's fees. Stripe credited it back on Sept 9, so it will appear as a credit in September.
2. **A $42.22 refund failed.** It was made on Aug 28 and is in August's refunds, but it failed on Sept 2 and the money came back to the balance. That customer probably hasn't received their money.
3. **Two won disputes don't match what was taken.** On one charge, $32.80 was withdrawn and $33.36 came back. On another, $20.28 was withdrawn and $20.09 came back. I used the amounts Stripe actually booked.
4. **About $192 of the sales tax sits inside refunded charges** (plus about $18 in disputes). The $8,402.50 is tax on the charges before those refunds, so the tax owed may be lower.
5. **Splitting the month by UTC instead of local time gives slightly different figures.** Gross charges would be about $100 lower, and the reserve would be $198.13 lower because the Aug 31 withholding was booked at 23:11 local time. Stripe's own reports use the account timezone, as I did.
