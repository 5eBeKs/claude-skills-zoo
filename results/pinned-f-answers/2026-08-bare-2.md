## August 2026 Stripe month-end

**August sales.** 4,266 successful live charges came to **$139,509.28** gross. That figure includes tax collected from customers. We refunded $3,204.12 across 104 refunds. Customers disputed 10 charges, and Stripe pulled back $289.21 for them. We won 2 earlier disputes and got $53.45 back. After those items, net volume was **$136,069.40**, before Stripe fees.

**What Stripe kept.** Stripe's fees were **$7,302.22** in total:
- $6,408.26 in card processing fees
- $150.00 in dispute fees, less $30.00 returned on the 2 disputes we won
- $773.96 in Stripe Billing and Stripe Tax usage fees

On top of the fees, Stripe began holding a 5% rolling reserve from Aug 24. By month end it was holding **$1,671.14**. That money isn't lost: each day's amount comes back 30 days later.

**What reached the bank.** 18 deposits totalling **$119,805.62** arrived in August. That includes the $3,552.26 payout Stripe sent on Jul 31, which landed on Aug 3.

**Still on its way.** Stripe sent a **$10,085.69** payout on Aug 31, and it arrived Sep 1. After it, **$4,442.14** remained in the Stripe balance. The balance at the start of August was $3,685.15.

The figures tie out: $3,685.15 opening balance + $136,069.40 net volume − $7,302.22 fees − $1,671.14 reserve + $3,688.05 failed payout returned − $130,027.10 payouts sent = $4,442.14 closing balance. The payout-by-payout detail matches that closing balance, and the charge totals match the payments export exactly.

**Things to know:**
- **One payout failed.** The $3,688.05 payout sent Aug 13 bounced on Aug 14 because the bank account number was invalid. Stripe returned the money and included it in the $31,351.45 payout that landed Aug 19. Check that the bank details on file are correct now.
- **Month end is taken as midnight Central Daylight Time (UTC−5).** That is the account's own time zone, which the export's local timestamps and daily reserve entries show. Cutting at midnight UTC instead would move a few items: gross would be $139,409.16 and the reserve $1,473.01.
- **Tax.** $8,402.50 is all tax collected. Of that, $2,282.71 is US sales tax and $6,119.79 is foreign VAT/GST (UK, EU countries and Canada). Foreign amounts were converted at each charge's own exchange rate, so treat that part as accurate to within a few cents.
- **Duplicate fee.** Stripe charged the Aug 10 Billing usage fee twice ($11.51). It credited this back on Sep 9, so it's included in August's fees.
- **Failed refund.** A $42.22 refund issued Aug 28 failed on Sep 2, and the money came back to us in September. It's still counted in August's refunds.
- **Test charges left out.** 13 test-mode charges from Aug 12 are in the payments export but aren't real money, so they're excluded.
