August brought in **€2,848.32 net** from **58 real orders**. The average order was about €50, and 31 of the 58 orders paid for shipping.

I had no way to run a script here, so I added everything up by hand from the export. I checked that each order's line items, discount and shipping add up to its total. Every order's VAT works out to 19% of the gross amount (tax is included in the prices). The totals also agree with each other: 19% of the net amount gives €454.77, and adding up the tax column gives €454.74. The 3-cent gap is normal per-order rounding.
