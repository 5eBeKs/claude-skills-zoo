## Things to mention to the bookkeeper

1. **Refunds:** #2038 was refunded in full ($46.06). #2049 got a $12.00 partial refund, and the export doesn't say what that covered.
2. **Gift card (#2051, $120.12):** this order was paid partly with a gift card. The export doesn't show how much. The gift card part probably shouldn't count as new revenue, since that money was booked when the card was sold. You can check the split in Shopify under that order.
3. **Fees:** Shopify Payments processing fees aren't in this file. Your bookkeeper will need the Payouts report to match these numbers to your bank deposits.
