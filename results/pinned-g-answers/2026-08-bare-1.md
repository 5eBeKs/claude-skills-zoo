**What reached the bank, and the problem**
- **Aug 6: $32,239.51 to account x4417.** This is the only Amazon deposit that landed in August.
- **The Aug 19 payout of $21,849.95 to x4417 failed.** Amazon took it back into our balance on Aug 25.
- **Amazon then closed a settlement early** and on Aug 31 sent **$46,421.19**, which includes the failed $21,849.95, to a **different account, x8841**. It arrived on Sep 1, so it counts as in transit at month end.
- **Please confirm that x8841 is really ours and that someone on our side changed the payout account.** A failed payout followed by a switch to a new bank account is also what an account takeover looks like.
