## Amazon month-end, September 2026

I checked every settlement file against the transaction reports and the bank list, and they all match. September covers 1–30 Sep on Pacific time, which is how Seller Central reports it.

**What we sold.** We had $112,613.64 of product sales across 3,040 orders (3,936 units) that shipped or posted in September. Customer refunds were $5,028.11. That figure includes one A-to-z claim for $119.98 and one chargeback for $19.99. That leaves net product sales of $107,585.53.
