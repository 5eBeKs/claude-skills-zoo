## August 2026 Amazon month-end

All figures are in US dollars. Days are Pacific time, the same as the Seller Central reports. The August transaction report matches the settlement files exactly, settlement by settlement and order by order. The money traced from the end of July to the end of August balances to the cent.

**What we sold.** Product sales were $104,709.12 across 2,889 orders. Refunds were $4,518.25. That figure includes one A-to-z claim ($44.99) and one card chargeback ($64.99). Net product sales were $100,190.87.

**What Amazon kept.**
- **Amazon fees: $40,616.33.** This is referral fees of $15,616.88, FBA fulfilment fees of $17,840.96, and per-item and other transaction fees of $2,153.37. It also includes $3,926.77 for shipping labels bought through Amazon, $1,001.11 in FBA storage, placement and removal fees, $37.25 in coupon fees and the $39.99 subscription.
- **Advertising: $10,826.11.**
- **Reimbursements: $222.06**, which Amazon paid us for lost, damaged and returned stock.

Other items that don't have a line of their own:
- Shipping charged to customers: +$4,191.78
- Gift wrap: +$104.79
- Promotions: −$1,325.33
- Sales tax: $7,539.11 net was collected and paid over by Amazon, so it nets to zero for us.

August's net activity comes to **$51,941.73**.

**What reached the bank.** Only **$32,239.51** arrived in August, on Aug 6 into the account ending 4417.
- **Failed payment:** Amazon sent $21,849.95 to the 4417 account on Aug 18. That payment was returned on Aug 25, and it is not on the bank's list.
- **Paid again after month end:** Amazon added the returned $21,849.95 into a $46,421.19 payment it sent on Aug 31 to the account ending 8841. The bank received it on **Sep 1**, so it counts as September cash.
- **Please check:** make sure the account ending 8841 is ours and that the change from 4417 was intended.

**What Amazon owes us at Aug 31.**
- **$46,421.19** was on its way to the bank (the Sep 1 deposit).
- **$22,646.47** was still in the Amazon account, not yet paid out. This is:
  - $22,825.97 of orders from Aug 19–31 that Amazon was still holding (it pays sales out only after delivery). They were paid in the Sep 17 deposit.
  - less a $179.50 debit from a short settlement on Aug 31. Amazon took it out of that next payment rather than charging our card.
- That makes **$69,067.66** owed, not counting the reserve.
- **Reserve:** Amazon was also holding a **$4,069.25** reserve. Including it, Amazon held **$73,136.91** of our money in total.

**How the balance moved.** On Jul 31 Amazon held $53,434.69 of ours ($49,054.47 plus a $4,380.22 reserve). Add August's net activity of $51,941.73 and take off the $32,239.51 deposited. That gives $73,136.91, which matches the total above.

**About the order count.** The 2,889 orders are those charged in August, which matches the sales figure. Counted by the date the customer ordered, August had 2,917 shipped orders plus 41 cancelled.
